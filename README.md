# Obelisk

Obelisk converts camera-scanned colour negatives into finished positives. It works from a
measured calibration of your film stock and scanning setup, so a whole roll lands at one
density and one colour. Film without a calibration converts with Lab auto, a lab-printer
style conversion that balances and exposes every frame on its own.

Website: [image.northharbourinstruments.com](https://image.northharbourinstruments.com)

## Download

**[Get the latest release](https://github.com/northharbour/obelisk-releases/releases/latest)**
and pick the file for your computer. The version is in the file name:

| File | Runs on |
|---|---|
| `Obelisk-<version>-macOS.dmg` | macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon (M1 and later). Intel Macs are not supported. |
| `Obelisk-Setup-<version>.exe` | Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit |

Release notes and earlier versions are on the
[Releases page](https://github.com/northharbour/obelisk-releases/releases).

## Install

**macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag Obelisk to Applications. The first time you open it,
macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**. The app
is signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows:** run the installer. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
**More info → Run anyway**.

## Free trial

Without a licence, Obelisk runs as a 10-day trial from its first launch. Everything works
except export: you can convert, grade and compare whole rolls, but saving TIFF and JPEG
files needs a licence. After 10 days the app asks for one.

To get a licence, write to [info@northharbourinstruments.com](mailto:info@northharbourinstruments.com).
Load it in **Help → Licence…**.

## Your images stay on your computer

Conversion and automatic orientation run entirely on your machine. Images are never
uploaded.
